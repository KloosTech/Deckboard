Deckboard

A free third-party Kanban client for Nextcloud Deck.

Open your Nextcloud Deck boards natively on Android, iOS and the desktop: drag and drop cards, labels, assignees, comments, sharing and activity. Sign in with your browser or an app password. Your data stays between the app and your own Nextcloud server.

  • Free and open source (Apache License 2.0)
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics
  • Credentials stored in the platform keychain
  • English and German

Deckboard is an independent third-party client. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nextcloud GmbH. Nextcloud is a trademark of Nextcloud GmbH.
